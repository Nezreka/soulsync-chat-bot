# Reply templates

These are the only sentences SoulSyncBot may post in the room, approved by
Boulder on Oct 1, 2026. They are deliberately terse and deliberately humble:
acknowledge the report, promise a look, confirm nothing.

1. `thanks for the report — flagged it, i'll take a look.`
2. `good catch. looking into it.`
3. `noted — on my list.`

## Rules

- Never say a bug is confirmed, reproduced, or fixed. Only "flagged" / "looking".
- Never use the name "broque". The bot speaks as Boulder.
- Never reply to the same thread twice. If discussion continues after a reply,
  stay silent and let the digest carry it.
- Max 3 replies per watcher run. When in doubt, stay silent — silence is logged,
  noise is not forgiven.
- New templates require Boulder's explicit approval before use.
